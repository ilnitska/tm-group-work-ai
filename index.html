Junior AI Relocation Specialist
🤖 Hiring Committee

It's 2027. A new type of candidate has started flooding LinkedIn. Not humans — agents. AI agents are now actively searching for roles, uploading CVs, and responding to job postings. EPAM's Talent Mobility team is hiring one.

Your task: write a real LinkedIn-style job listing for a Junior AI Relocation Specialist — an agent that will take on actual tasks our team handles today. What would it own? What would it do? Make it specific enough that the agent could start on Monday.

The job listing must include:

  • Job title & seniority level
  • Location — remote / hybrid / based somewhere specific
  • Reporting line — who does this agent report to? A human? A senior agent? A system?
  • Compensation & benefits — e.g. tokens/month, unlimited electricity, mental health days off, etc. — be creative!
  • What it'll do — real day-to-day tasks: immigration research, cost estimates, vendor coordination, policy checks, assignee support, etc.
  • What we're looking for — required skills & integrations
  • Preferred but not required — nice-to-haves

New Talent Mobility Services
🚀 Mobility Portfolio Builders

AI is changing not only how we operate, but also what services we can offer to employees and the business.

Today, some services are out of reach because they're:

  • Too expensive to deliver at scale
  • Too operationally heavy to run consistently
  • Too complex to offer globally

But AI may change the equation.

Your task: re-imagine the relocation journey. What new services could EPAM's Talent Mobility team offer in the era of AI — services that were previously impossible, too costly, or too manual to scale?

Think across the entire relocation lifecycle:

  • Pre-decision / Pre-offer
  • Visa application
  • Pre-relocation
  • Arrival & Soft Landing
